Billing & Payment

Learn how to manage customer accounts, track unpaid invoices, and process refunds or sales returns.

Invoice & Sales Management

Invoice List: View all generated invoices from POS sales, track their payment statuses, and record incoming payments.

Invoice Status Indicators

Fully Paid (Green)

The total amount has been settled. No further action is required.

Unpaid (Red)

The invoice is pending payment. Click the invoice to add a payment entry when the customer pays.

Partial (Orange)

The customer has paid a portion, but a balance remains to be settled.

Customer Ledger

Account Tracking: Keep track of regular clients, their credit limits, and their overall balance.

Ledger Table Information

Customer Profile

Name and contact details of your registered clients.

Total Outstanding

The accumulated amount they currently owe the store across all unpaid invoices.

View History

Click the "View Ledger" button to see a chronological breakdown of their purchases and payments.

Sales Returns

Handling Returns: Process items returned by customers due to damage or wrong variations.

Return Process

1. Initiate Return

Search for the specific Invoice number and select the item the customer is returning.

2. Condition Check

Choose whether the returned item should go back into inventory (good condition) or be marked as waste (damaged).

3. Refund Method

Select if the customer will receive a Cash Refund or if it will be applied as Store Credit.
