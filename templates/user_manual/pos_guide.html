Point of Sale (POS)

Select a submodule from the dropdown list below to view instructions on processing customer transactions, creating quotations, and printing receipts.

POS Interface & Cart

Main POS Screen: Add products to the customer's cart, adjust quantities, and scan barcodes for fast checkout.

Cart Layout Details

Product Grid

Click on any item in the visual grid to instantly add it to the active transaction cart.

Barcode Scanner

Ensure your cursor is in the search box to scan physical items. They will be added to the cart automatically.

Quantity & Remove

Use the +/- buttons in the cart to change amounts. Click the red 'X' to remove an item.

Cart Controls

Clear Cart

Instantly voids all items currently in the cart to start a fresh transaction.

Processing Payments

Checkout Process: Finalize the transaction by selecting the appropriate payment method. Follow the steps below for each mode.

How to Use Payment Modes

Cash Payment

Step 1: Select Cash as the payment method.

Step 2: Type the exact Amount Tendered given by the customer in the input field.

Step 3: The system will automatically compute and display the Change Amount.

Step 4: Click Confirm to complete the sale and print the receipt.

Card / E-Wallet

Step 1: Select Card or E-Wallet (e.g., GCash, Maya).

Step 2: Process the customer's payment on your physical terminal or phone app first.

Step 3: Once successful, type the Reference/Trace Number into the system for tracking and auditing.

Step 4: Click Confirm to finalize.

Store Credit (Utang)

Step 1: Select Store Credit for trusted clients.

Step 2: Search and select the Customer's Name from the dropdown registry.

Step 3: Verify that the total does not exceed their allowable credit limit.

Step 4: Click Confirm. The transaction will be sent to the Billing Module as an Unpaid Invoice for future collection.

Quotations & Confirming Payments

Post-Transaction: Save cart data as a quotation for undecided customers, or confirm the payment to complete the sale and generate a receipt.

Action Buttons

Save as Quotation

Holds the transaction without affecting inventory. You can load this quotation later if the customer decides to buy.

Confirm Payment

Finalizes the transaction, records the sale, and generates the receipt for the customer.
