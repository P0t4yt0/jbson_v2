Reports & Analytics

Monitor your business performance, track daily sales, check supplier expenses, and generate overall records with ease. Select a category below to learn more.

Common Report Tools (Filters & Exports)

Standard Controls: Most reports share these simple tools to help you find and save the exact data you need.

Filter by Date

Select a start and end date, then click "Generate" to only show data for a specific day, week, or month. Click "Clear" to reset.

Export PDF

Click the red "Export PDF" button to download a printable, clean document of your current report.

Export CSV

Click the green "Export CSV" button to download the data for Excel or Google Sheets so you can edit it later.

Sales & Returns Report

Sales Activity: View all the money coming in, the total number of customer purchases, and any returned items.

Key Sales Numbers

The top colored boxes give you an instant summary of your business sales.

Total Gross Sales

The overall total amount collected from all customer purchases before any deductions.

Net Sales

The true income after subtracting the refunded money from returned items.

Total Returns

The total value of items that customers brought back for a refund (shown as a negative number).

Transactions

The total number of receipts or individual sales completed during the selected dates.

Breakdowns & Logs

Payment Breakdown

Shows how customers paid you—whether by Cash, Online Wallet, Credit, or Bank Transfer.

Top Revenue Products

A leaderboard showing which items are making the most money for the store.

Transaction Breakdown

A complete list of every receipt generated. Use the search bar to find a specific receipt number.

Sales Return Logs

A record of every refunded item, showing the original receipt and how much was returned to the customer.

Purchase & Expenses Report

Business Spending: Monitor how much money goes out of the store to buy supplies and inventory.

Expense Tracking Features

Total Purchase (Expenses)

The total amount of money you spent paying suppliers for inventory.

Total Orders

The overall count of Purchase Orders you sent to your suppliers.

Supplier Expense Breakdown

Shows exactly how much money went to each specific supplier (e.g., Jewelle, Denden).

Most Purchased Products

A leaderboard of items you order the most frequently for restocking.

Purchase Order Logs

The bottom table tracks all your individual purchase orders and their delivery statuses.

PENDING (Yellow)

The order has been created but the supplier hasn't delivered the items yet.

RECEIVED (Green)

The items have arrived, and the inventory stock has been updated.

Inventory & Product Report

Stock Valuation: Check the total worth of the items currently inside your store and get alerts for low supplies.

Inventory Metrics

Total Inventory Value

The total potential selling price of every single item currently available in your store.

Total Product Types

The total count of unique items registered in your system.

Low Stock Warning

Instantly shows how many items are running out so you can plan your next bulk order.

Detailed Stock Valuation Table

This list shows every item in your system, categorized properly. Watch out for items with the Low Stock warning.

Low Stock Badge

Items that have reached zero or critical levels are tagged with a red LOW STOCK badge under their name.

Invoice Report

Customer Billing: Keep track of customer invoices, identify who has paid, and see who still owes you money.

Invoice Summaries

Total Invoiced

The total amount billed to customers, combining both paid and unpaid invoices.

Total Paid

The actual cash already collected from finished invoices.

Total Balance Due

The total remaining amount that customers still need to pay to the store.

Detailed Invoice Logs

Use this table to check the status of specific customers.

PAID Status

A green PAID tag means the customer has fully settled their bill and has zero balance.

UNPAID Status

A red UNPAID tag means you still need to collect the remaining balance from the customer.

Procurement & Supplier Report

Supplier Management: Analyze your relationships with suppliers, track bulk orders, and see pending deliveries.

Procurement Highlights

Total Procurement Spend

Your overall spending limit or total amount used for all bulk supplier orders.

Pending Deliveries (Value)

The cash value of inventory items that you have already ordered but are still waiting to be delivered.

Total POs Issued

The number of formal Purchase Order requests generated by the system.

Supplier Tables

Supplier Spend Leaderboard

A quick view ranking your top suppliers based on how many orders you placed and how much you paid them.

Master Purchase History

A consolidated record of all past and ongoing requests. Use the search bar to find an old PO number instantly.

Profit & Loss Report

Business Earnings: Understand your true earnings by subtracting all costs and expenses from your overall sales.

Profit Computation

Gross Sales

The total money coming in from customer purchases.

Inventory Cost

The amount deducted for buying stocks and supplies from your suppliers.

Operating Expenses

The amount deducted for everyday business bills like payroll, electricity, and rent.

Net Profit

Your actual take-home money after all costs and expenses are subtracted from your sales.

Expense and Purchase Logs

Use the tables below the summary boxes to track exactly where your money is going.

Purchase Cost Logs

A list of all payments made to suppliers for inventory. Click + New PO to draft a new purchase order directly.

Operating Expense Logs

A list of regular business bills (like payroll or utilities). Click + Add Expense to manually log a new store expense.

Annual Business Report

Yearly Overview: A big-picture view of your business performance over the entire year, complete with visual charts and health indicators.

Annual Highlights & Trends

Financial Performance Trend

A visual chart that makes it easy to compare your sales (blue bar) against your expenses (red bar) and profit trend line month-by-month.

System Status

A quick tag (e.g., green PROFITABLE) that tells you if your business is currently making or losing money overall.

Master Logs & Business Health

Monthly Breakdown Logs

A clean table showing a summary of your earnings and spending for each specific month.

Total Gross & Outflow

Located at the bottom, this compares all money that entered the business versus all money that left the business.

Profit Margin & Expense Ratio

Shows the percentage of return or cost per every peso earned, helping you gauge if your pricing is effective.
