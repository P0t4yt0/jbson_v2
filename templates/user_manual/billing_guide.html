Billing & Payment

Learn how to manage trade credit accounts, track invoices, process payments, and handle sales returns.

Invoice Management

Invoice List: View all generated invoices from POS sales, track their payment statuses, and record incoming payments.

Invoice Status Indicators

Fully Paid

The total amount has been settled. No further collection action is required.

Unpaid

The invoice is pending payment. Access the invoice to add a payment entry when the customer pays.

Trade Credit Accounts

Account Tracking: Keep track of regular clients, their trade credit accounts, and their overall outstanding balance.

Account Table Information

Customer Profile

Name and contact details of your registered clients who utilize trade credit.

Total Outstanding

The accumulated amount they currently owe the store across all unpaid invoices.

View History

Click the action button to see a chronological breakdown of their purchases and payments.

Sales Returns

Handling Returns: Process items returned by customers by pulling up their original transaction, selecting the items, and recording the refunded amount.

Return Process

1. Initiate Return

Click the Process New Return button and input the customer's original Transaction ID to pull up their purchase record.

2. Select Products

Once the transaction loads on your screen, select the specific products that the customer is returning.

3. Confirm & Refund

Click Confirm to finalize the return. The system will automatically calculate the total and record the refunded amount.

Return History

View Return Details

All completed returns are saved in the Return History table. You can check this list anytime to review past refunded amounts and the specific items returned.
