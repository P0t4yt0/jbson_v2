Inventory Management

Select a submodule from the dropdown list below to view its specific features, guidelines, and user interface details.

Products & Registration

Product Management: View and manage your inventory products, prices, and stock levels.

Product Table Information

The main table displays your current inventory data. Here is what each column means:

ID & Product Name

The special code and the exact name of your item.

Supplier & Category

Shows who supplied the item and its group (like Power Tools).

Price & Barcode

The selling price and its scannable barcode number.

QTY & Actions

Shows how many pieces are left. Green means good, red means low stock. Click the pencil icon to edit.

Product Priority Indicators

Beside the product names, you will see small colored dots. These tell you how important it is to restock the item.

Green (Always Instock - A)

These are top priority items. You must make sure they are always available in your store.

Yellow (Regular Check - B)

These are medium priority items. You just need to check and monitor their stock levels regularly.

Grey (Bulk Order - C)

These are low priority items. They are usually restocked by ordering in large bulk quantities.

Product Overview Buttons

View Low Stock

Click this to easily see the items that are running out of stock.

Analyze Priority

Shows you which items you need to restock immediately based on what sells fast.

Auto-Calibrate ROP

Automatically computes when you should reorder items so you don't run out of supplies.

Scan Barcode (Search)

Click the "Scan Barcode" button beside the search bar to quickly find a product using your barcode scanner.

Parts of "Add Product"

When adding a new product, you need to fill up the form step-by-step.

1. Basic Information

Here you will type the required details like the Product Name, Category, Supplier, and Price.

2. Safety Stock (Optional)

Input the Unit Cost and Estimated Daily/Yearly Sales to help track your business performance.

3. Delivery Lead Time

Put the Lead Time in days. This tells the system how long it takes for the supplier to deliver the item.

Form Action Buttons

Scan Barcode (Input)

Click this beside the Barcode ID field to use your scanner and automatically type the item's code.

Confirm & Cancel

Click "Confirm" to save the new inventory item, or "Cancel" to remove your draft.

Category Management

Category Management: Organize your items into groups (like Hardware, Wood, or Paint).

Category List Details

Each row represents a saved category. Here is the information displayed:

Category Name

The name of the group, like "Hardware" or "Paint".

Prefix Code

A short code (like "HW") so the system can easily tag items in this group.

Items Count

Shows how many items are currently saved inside this category.

Category Action Buttons

View Products

Click the "View Products" dropdown button to see a quick list of all the hardware items saved under this category.

Edit (Pencil Icon)

Click the pencil icon if you want to change the name or the prefix code of the category.

Delete (Trash Icon)

Click the red trash can icon to completely remove this category from your system.

Adding a New Category

Click "Add Category" at the top right to open this popup window.

Save & Cancel

Type the new category name, then click "Save" to apply it to your system, or "Cancel" to close the window.

Purchase Order Hub

Purchase Order Hub: Draft and manage orders that you want to buy from your suppliers.

Parts of the Purchase Order Page

1. Order History

Check the left panel to see your past orders. It will show "PENDING" if items haven't arrived, or "RECEIVED" if completed.

2. Select Supplier & Date

Choose which supplier you are ordering from, and click the calendar to pick your Expected Delivery Date.

3. Order Items

Select the product you want to buy, type the quantity, and check the unit cost. The Total will automatically compute below.

Purchase Order Action Buttons

Smart Draft / Manual

Click "Smart Draft" to let the system suggest what to order based on low stocks. Click "+ Manual" to start a blank order.

Add Another Item

Click this to insert a new row so you can include more items in the same supplier purchase order.

Save or Generate

Click "Save as Draft" if you want to finish the order later. Click "Generate" to finalize and send it.

Barcode Generation

Barcode Generation: Create a new barcode for items without an existing code.

Generation Process

Generate New Barcode

Type the product name first, then click this button to instantly create a scannable barcode pattern.

Generation Successful

Once done, a success message will pop up showing the new barcode and its generated number.

Print Label

Click the "Print Label" button below the barcode to directly print the sticker.

Copy Code

Click the yellow "Copy Code" button so you can easily paste the number to the Add Product form.

Barcode History List

At the bottom of the page, you will see the Barcode History table. It records the Date Generated, Product Name, and the Barcode ID.

Copy Icon

Click the copy icon under the Action column to quickly copy the barcode number.

Print Icon

Click the printer icon under the Action column to easily reprint old barcode stickers.

Delete Icon

Click the red trash can icon to remove old or incorrect barcodes from your history list.
