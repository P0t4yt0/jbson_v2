Dashboard Overview

Welcome to your control center! Here is a quick look at the entire dashboard before we break down each specific feature below.

1. Quick Action Buttons

Located at the top right of your screen. These shortcuts help you navigate and manage tasks quickly.

Add Product

Click this button to quickly add a new item to your store inventory.

POS (Point of Sale)

Opens the checkout screen to process new customer sales and transactions instantly.

Notifications

Displays a red counter badge when you have active system alerts or stock reminders.

2. Main Dashboard & Financial Data

Provides a real-time overview of your store's total health and performance records.

Gross Sales

The total amount of money collected from all completed customer purchases.

Net Profit

Your clean earnings. This is calculated after deducting product costs and active expenses.

Cash Outflow

The total sum spent on inventory restocking payments and business expenses.

Unpaid Receivables

Pending credit balances or tracked money that customers still owe to your store.

Date Filter

Change the date range (e.g., Today, All Time) for the displayed metrics and charts.

Performance Charts

Visual trends monitoring your 7-Day Financial overview and Top Selling products.

3. Important Alerts

Critical logs placed at the dashboard requiring your immediate business attention.

Critical Stock

Lists item names running critically low or marked out of stock. Click "View All" to manage purchase restocks.

Due Invoices

Monitors delayed credit payouts. Click "Collect" to register clear customer payments.
