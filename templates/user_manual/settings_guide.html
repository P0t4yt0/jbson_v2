General Settings

Manage your system maintenance, backup your important files, and secure your data easily. Here is a full look at the settings page.

1. System Maintenance

Keep your system running smoothly by checking for any internal errors.

Start System Check

Click this button to run a quick check. The system will review its performance and make sure everything is working properly without any hidden problems.

2. Backup & Recovery

Always ensure your business records are safe. Generate highly-secured backups or upload encrypted files to restore the system state.

Encrypted System Backup

To safely backup your database, enter an encryption password of your choice in the field provided, then click Encrypt & Download.

System Recovery

If you need to revert or restore data, click Choose File to upload your `.enc` backup file. Enter the exact decryption password you used during backup, then click Decrypt & Restore.

3. Danger Zone

Strict warnings apply here. Only use this area if you are completely resetting the store.

Wipe All Data

Clicking this red button will permanently delete all your inventory stocks and transaction records. This action cannot be undone, so use it with extreme caution.
